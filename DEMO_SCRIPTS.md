# Sanctuary: Spiritual Gifts Demo Scripts

Use these scripts to record professional demo videos for your church stakeholders. 

## Preparation
1. Open the App in its own tab using the "Open in new tab" icon.
2. Ensure you are logged in as an Admin (click the Shield icon in bottom-right).
3. Use a screen recording tool (like Loom, QuickTime, or OBS).

---

## Video 1: Completing the Discovery Journey (User Flow)
**Objective:** Show how easy and inspiring it is for a congregant to take the survey.

*   **Scene 1 (Hero Page):** Start on the landing page. 
    *   *Script:* "Welcome to Sanctuary's Spiritual Gifts Discovery. We've designed a beautiful, minimalist experience to help our community find their God-given purpose."
*   **Scene 2 (Taking the Survey):** Click 'Begin the Journey' and answer 3-4 questions. Show the smooth transitions.
    *   *Script:* "The survey is intuitive and reflective. It guides users through questions that map to their unique strengths."
*   **Scene 3 (Final Step):** Enter a name and email.
    *   *Script:* "Once finished, we collect the user's details to secure their results and send them a personal summary."
*   **Scene 4 (Results Page):** Scroll through the gifts and the 'Join a Team' CTA.
    *   *Script:* "The results don't just identify gifts—they invite the user into action, connecting them directly with service teams where they can make an impact."

---

## Video 2: Expanding the Library (Adding Gifts)
**Objective:** Show how church leaders can add new spiritual categories.

*   **Scene 1 (Dashboard):** Start on the Admin Dashboard under 'Spiritual Gifts'.
    *   *Script:* "Our system is dynamic. As our ministry grows, cursors can easily add new gift definitions."
*   **Scene 2 (Form):** Click 'New Gift' and fill out a sample (e.g., 'Hospitality' or 'Craftsmanship').
    *   *Script:* "Simply define the gift, provide a scriptural basis, and describe how it manifests in our community."
*   **Scene 3 (Success):** Click 'Save' and see it appear in the list.
    *   *Script:* "Instantly, these gifts become part of the discovery pool available to all users."

---

## Video 3: Refining the Survey (Adding Questions)
**Objective:** Show how to update the question pool.

*   **Scene 1 (Questions Tab):** Switch to 'Survey Questions' in the admin panel.
    *   *Script:* "We can constantly refine our survey to ensure it speaks the language of our congregation."
*   **Scene 2 (Edit/New):** Click 'New Question' and type a prompt. 
    *   *Script:* "Adding a new question is as simple as typing the prompt our users will see."
*   **Scene 3 (Mapping):** Show the dropdown where you select which gift this question evaluates.
    *   *Script:* "Each question is mapped to a specific gift, ensuring accurate and meaningful results for every individual."

---

## Video 4: Data-Driven Ministry (Analytics & Mapping)
**Objective:** Show stakeholders that we are measuring effectiveness.

*   **Scene 1 (Analytics Tab):** Show the 'Operational Insights' page.
    *   *Script:* "Beyond the individual journey, we track how our congregation is engaging. We can see exactly how many people start their journey versus how many join a service team."
*   **Scene 2 (Mapping Overview):** Briefly highlight the 'Mapped Gift' column in the questions list.
    *   *Script:* "This direct mapping between discovery and service allows us to manage our ministry with clarity and grace."
